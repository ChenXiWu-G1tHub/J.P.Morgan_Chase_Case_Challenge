Step 1 of 4

Your first investment, in familiar terms

1

Name a goal

Like a labelled savings jar, e.g. holiday fund, £2,000.

2

Choose a basket

A fund holds many companies, so one bad apple matters less.

3

Set a standing order

Auto-invest works like a gym direct debit.

4

Check in monthly

Review progress, not daily prices. Dips are normal.

Check your understanding

Can you say it in one sentence?

I am putting £[amount] a month into [pot], which owns [what]. It could go up or down because [why].

If you can't, pause and learn more. Also ask: how does it earn, what can go wrong, how do I get out?
