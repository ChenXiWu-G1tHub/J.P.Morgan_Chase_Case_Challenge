Food shops are companies you could own

People buy food in good times and bad, so this area is often called steady. A fund can hold many food and household companies, giving you small slices of firms you already pay every week.

The idea: a basket of everyday-goods companies [Fund from live feed]

A busy shop is not a promise its share price rises. You can earn if prices rise or companies pay a share of profit (a dividend), and you can lose if they fall. Holding many companies lowers the risk of one failing, but does not remove it.
